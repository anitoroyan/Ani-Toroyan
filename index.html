About Me

Building digital experiences, one detail at a time.

I'm Ani Toroyan, a Shopify developer and web designer interested in creating websites that feel considered, easy to navigate, and visually consistent.

My work focuses on Shopify stores, custom landing pages, and HTML/CSS website layouts. I enjoy turning an idea into a structured digital experience, from the overall layout to the smaller details that make a page feel complete.

This portfolio is the starting point of my public project collection. It brings together my Shopify store and two landing pages, with more work to be added as I continue to build.

My Approach

From first idea to finished page.

A straightforward process helps keep every project organized, consistent, and focused on its purpose.

01

Understand the Goal

Identify the purpose of the website, its audience, and the information visitors need first.

02

Structure the Experience

Organize content into a clear layout with considered hierarchy, spacing, and navigation.

03

Build & Refine

Develop the page, refine the visual details, and check that the layout adapts to different screen sizes.

04

Review the Details

Check links, content, spacing, and consistency before presenting the finished experience.
